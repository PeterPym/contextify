# Hacker News Official Guidelines

## General Guidelines

Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html

### What to Submit

**On-Topic:** Material that intellectually engages hackers broadly, not just coding and startups. The core principle: "anything that gratifies one's intellectual curiosity."

**Off-Topic:** Political stories, crime coverage, sports, celebrity news (unless revealing novel phenomena), slapstick videos, and animal content. Generally, if television news would cover it, it belongs elsewhere.

### Submission Standards

- Avoid formatting tricks like ALL CAPS or exclamation marks to draw attention
- Link to original sources, not secondary reports
- Don't use HN primarily for self-promotion
- Remove site names from titles (they appear automatically)
- Simplify numbered listicles ("10 Ways To Do X" -> "How To Do X")
- Use original titles unless misleading
- Tag videos and PDFs with [video] or [pdf]
- Don't contact HN through posts; use hn@ycombinator.com
- Avoid deleting and reposting
- Never solicit votes or comments

### Comment Etiquette

- Maintain kindness; avoid snark and cross-examination
- Respond to arguments, not people ("1 + 1 is 2" not "You're wrong")
- Offer thoughtful criticism, not rigid negativity
- Assume good faith; interpret charitably
- Skip shallow dismissals and internet cliches
- Avoid political battles, they undermine curiosity
- Don't debate whether others read articles
- Use asterisks for emphasis instead of CAPS
- Flag spam rather than arguing in threads
- Avoid complaints about astroturfing or voting patterns

## Show HN Guidelines

Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html

### Purpose

"Show HN is for something you've made that other people can play with." Users can test projects, provide feedback, and discuss them in threads.

### What's On Topic

Acceptable submissions include interactive projects users can run locally or physical items they can handle. Hardware posts may include videos or detailed articles. Book contributions can feature sample chapters.

### What's Off Topic

Blog posts, signup pages, newsletters, reading lists, and similar content cannot be demonstrated, so they don't qualify. Submit these as regular posts instead.

### Requirements for Posters

Your project must be personally created work you're available to discuss. Early-stage projects are welcome - polish isn't required. Remove barriers to trying your work; avoid mandatory signups or email requirements to maximize feedback.

Only submit when your work is ready for user testing. Landing pages and fundraising pitches aren't appropriate.

### Posting Instructions

Begin your submission title with "Show HN". New posts appear on the shownew page initially, then graduate to the main show page after reaching a points threshold.

### What Doesn't Qualify

Minor version updates typically lack sufficient substance. Major overhauls are more appropriate.

### Community Guidelines for Comments

Treat contributors with respect. Ask genuine questions rather than interrogatively challenging them. Offer constructive suggestions instead of criticism. Support learning by helping others improve their skills.

## Formatting Options

HN has limited markdown support:

- **Paragraphs:** Blank lines separate paragraphs
- **Italics:** Text surrounded by asterisks is italicized
- **Literal asterisk:** Use `\*` or `**` to get a literal asterisk
- **Code/verbatim:** Text after a blank line that is indented by two or more spaces is reproduced verbatim (intended for code)
- **Links:** URLs become links automatically, except in the text field of a submission
- **Fixing links:** If your URL gets linked incorrectly, put it in `<angle brackets>` and it should work

**Note:** No bold, no headers, no bullet points, no inline code backticks. Keep it simple.
