# Show HN Moderation Guidance from dang

Extracted from HN moderator "dang" comments about Show HN conduct (2014-2025).

Source: HN Algolia search for comments by dang mentioning "show hn"

---

## Core Rules (Repeatedly Enforced)

### 1. Must Have Something to Try NOW

> "Show HN is explicitly not for email signups. You have to have something for people to try now."

> "Show HN requires something people can play with now."

> "Signup sites aren't enough to make a Show HN."

> "An email signup cannot be a Show HN. Please read the rules."

> "The Show HN guidelines explicitly ask for more than just an email signup."

**Key point:** The #1 reason dang removes "Show HN" from titles is landing pages / email signups without a working product.

### 2. Must Be Your Own Work

> "Did you make this? Show HN is for showing your own work."

> "Please don't put 'Show HN' in the title unless you're personally involved."

> "'Show HN' implies that you did [make this]."

### 3. Must Be Ready for Feedback

> "Only submit when your work is ready for user testing."

> "You can't make something a Show HN until something people can actually try out."

> "Make sure to do the Show HN when it's ready."

---

## What Gets "Show HN" Removed from Titles

dang actively removes "Show HN" from titles when posts don't qualify:

- **Email signups / landing pages:** "We took 'Show HN' out of the title because this appears to be an email signup."
- **Blog posts:** "Lecture slides are not a Show HN. Blog posts don't count as Show HNs."
- **Fundraisers:** "Show HNs can't be fundraisers."
- **Ads:** "An ad is not a Show HN. Please don't do that again."
- **Others' work:** "You wouldn't be penalized, except you shouldn't put 'Show HN' if it isn't your own work."
- **Minor updates:** "That's for when you're introducing a project, not adding new features."

---

## What Gets "Show HN" Added to Titles

dang sometimes adds "Show HN" to titles when posts qualify but didn't use it:

> "Since this looks like people can try it and give feedback, we added 'Show HN'."

> "We added 'Show HN' to the title because it looks like a perfect candidate."

> "Ok, we'll put 'Show HN' back."

## Comment Conduct in Show HN Threads

### Be Respectful

> "This comment breaks the Show HN guidelines. Be respectful. Ask questions out of curiosity. Don't cross-examine."

> "Please don't post comments like this. The combination of negativity and pedantry goes against the intent of the site."

> "A few disagreements have crossed over into being disrespectful. You can disagree without calling names."

### Engage Constructively

> "A good Show HN discussion should engage with a project at its own level."

> "Treat contributors with respect. Ask genuine questions rather than interrogatively challenging them."

---

## Reposting Rules

> "Reposts in general are ok once or twice if a story hasn't had significant attention."

> "After a year or so, reposts are fine."

> "Previously discussed projects [shouldn't be] Show HN" (if already had attention).

---

## Voting Rings / Solicitation

dang actively monitors for vote manipulation:

> "Never solicit votes or comments."

> Warning about "voting rings and beta signup exclusion."

> Enforcement of guidelines on vote collusion.

---

## Technical Notes

> "Posts without URLs get penalized. You'd be better off reposting this with a link to your site."

> "Show HN" posts appear on `/shownew` first, graduate to `/show` after points threshold.

---

## Summary: What dang Enforces Most

1. **No email signups** - must have working product
2. **Your own work** - not curating others' projects
3. **Respectful comments** - no cross-examination or snark
4. **No vote solicitation** - earns bans
5. **No repeat Show HNs** - unless significant time/changes
