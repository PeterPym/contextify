## Title Pattern Analysis

### What Works: Personal Story + Result

The highest-performing posts often lead with personal context:
- "I made..." / "I built..." / "I created..."
- "I got laid off and..." / "I spent 6 years..."
- "I was curious about X, ended up making..."

### What Works: Surprising/Playful Concepts

- "Tetris in a PDF"
- "Tinder but only pictures of my wife"
- "Find best bus seat to avoid the sun"
- "I built a house optimized for LAN parties"

### What Works: Clear Problem/Solution

- "A dedicated scratchpad for developers"
- "Open-source investment tracker"
- "Run any GUI app in terminal"

### What Works: Nostalgia/Cultural References

- "Clippy - 90s UI for local LLMs"
- "I recreated Windows XP as my portfolio"
- "CSS library based on Counter Strike 1.6 UI"
