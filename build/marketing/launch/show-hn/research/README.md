## Key Takeaways

### What Makes a Valid Show HN

1. **Must be something people can try NOW** - no landing pages, no email signups
2. **Must be your own work** - don't post others' projects
3. **Must be ready for feedback** - early stage is fine, but functional

### Title Format

```
Show HN: [Product Name] - [What it does in ~10 words]
```

### Comment Conduct

- Be respectful to creators showing their work
- Ask genuine questions, don't cross-examine
- Offer constructive suggestions, not criticism
- Support learning and improvement

### What NOT to Post

- Email signup pages
- Landing pages without product
- Blog posts
- Fundraising pitches
- Minor version updates
- Others' work
