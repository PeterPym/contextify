💡 HOW IT WORKS

Contextify runs as a lightweight native macOS HUD that monitors your ~/.claude/projects/ and ~/.codex/sessions/ directories. When you start a conversation with Claude Code or Codex, Contextify automatically detects it, tracks the conversation timeline in real-time, and generates intelligent summaries using advanced LLM processing.

All data is stored locally in a SQLite database with full support for custom locations (Dropbox, iCloud Drive). Your conversations remain private and under your control—nothing leaves your Mac.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
