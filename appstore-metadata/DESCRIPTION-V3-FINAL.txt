💡 HOW IT WORKS

Contextify monitors your ~/.claude/projects/ and ~/.codex/sessions/ directories in real-time. When you start a conversation, it automatically ingests the transcript, generates summaries, and adds everything to your permanent database.

All data lives in a local SQLite database with support for custom locations (Dropbox, iCloud Drive). Your conversations remain private—nothing is transmitted to external servers.
